# Using Keystone with Phasmophobia

## Ghost identifier

**Library → Ghost identifier** works like the journal's evidence page, with the maths done for you.

- Tap a piece of evidence once when you find it (**✓**), again when you've ruled it out (**✕**), and once more to clear it.
- Set **Evidence the ghost gives** to match the difficulty: 3 up to Professional, 2 on Nightmare, 1 on Insanity. On harder difficulties a missing piece might just be hidden, and the identifier allows for that; pieces a ghost always shows (the Moroi's Spirit Box, the Goryo's D.O.T.S.) still count.
- **Worth checking** marks the evidence that would split what's left.
- **Rule out** a ghost by its behaviour (hunt speed, a ghost event it can't do). It stays struck until you bring it back or start a new contract.
- **New contract** clears everything but the difficulty.

The same identifier is on your phone (the Keystone home-screen app) and in the overlay's **Identifier** panel, so whoever's on the camera can tick evidence while you walk.

## Timers

The Overlay tab's Timers section has one-tap timers for incense: 90 seconds, 180 for a Spirit, 60 for a Demon, and the 25-second gap after a hunt ends. Your phone has the same buttons. Tapping one again restarts it.

## Overlay

Run Phasmophobia in **borderless** or **windowed** mode (Options → Graphics) so the overlay shows on top. It never touches the game, so it's fine in any lobby.

## Mods

Mods are single-player only. Keystone keeps them off until you turn them on for this game, and they should only be used in a solo, offline contract: in multiplayer they spoil the game for everyone else and can get your account banned.
