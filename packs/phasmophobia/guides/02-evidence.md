# Evidence, at a glance

Every ghost leaves three of these seven, unless the difficulty hides some.

| Evidence | How to get it |
| --- | --- |
| EMF Level 5 | A level 5 reading on the EMF Reader |
| D.O.T.S. Projector | A ghostly figure moving through the green D.O.T.S. lights |
| Ultraviolet | Fingerprints, handprints or footprints under the UV light |
| Freezing Temperatures | A thermometer reading below 0°C / 32°F |
| Ghost Orb | Floating orbs seen through a video camera or head camera |
| Ghost Writing | Writing in a ghost writing book you've put down |
| Spirit Box | A reply on the spirit box after asking a question |

## Difficulty

- Up to **Professional**: all three pieces.
- **Nightmare**: two; one is hidden at random.
- **Insanity**: one.

Some ghosts always show one particular piece even when others are hidden: the Deogen and Moroi (Spirit Box), Goryo (D.O.T.S.), Hantu (Freezing Temperatures) and Obake (Ultraviolet). The Mimic shows fake Ghost Orbs on top of its three, even when no evidence is given.

The **Ghosts** section of the Library has every ghost's evidence, strengths and weaknesses, from the Phasmophobia Wiki.
