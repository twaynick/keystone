# Codes in LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga

## Entering a code

1. Go to the **Mos Eisley Cantina** (the hub between levels).
2. Walk up to the counter and open the shop, then choose **Enter Code**.
3. Type the code. It becomes available in the shop: buy it there with studs.

Extras (score multipliers, Super Gonk and the rest) are switched on from the pause menu once bought. Score multipliers stack.

## The codes

Keystone lists 61 codes under **Library → Codes**, each with a Copy button. Only codes that at least two separate guides agree on are included, so a typo in one guide can't send you a dud.

The **Codes entered** checklist (Overlay tab) lets you tick off characters and vehicles as you unlock them; pick it as the checklist to see it in the overlay and on your phone.

## Overlay

Run the game in **borderless** or **windowed** mode for Keystone's overlay and browser (**Ctrl+Shift+B**) to show on top.
