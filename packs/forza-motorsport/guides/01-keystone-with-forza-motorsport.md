# Using Keystone with Forza Motorsport

Forza Motorsport needs an internet connection for any progression: career, credits, car levels and new cars. A game where everything that matters happens online is one Keystone treats as online-only, so it never opens the game's memory and there's no Mods tab. Turn 10 can ban accounts for cheating, and that isn't worth the risk for a game you can't progress offline.

What you do get:

## Library

All 680 cars, grouped by make, each with its picture, class and Performance Index (PI), value, drivetrain layout, country and the game's stat bars. Search finds a car by any part of its name. Open a car for its Forza Wiki page in Keystone's browser (**Ctrl+Shift+B** shows it over the game).

Classes go by PI: E (100–300), D (301–400), C (401–500), B (501–600), A (601–700), S (701–800), R (801–900), P (901–998) and X (999).

## Overlay

Set **Display mode** to Windowed or Borderless in the game's video options so the overlay, timers and browser show on top. Timers are handy for practice stints and tyre or fuel planning; checklists for the Builders' Cup tours you've finished.
