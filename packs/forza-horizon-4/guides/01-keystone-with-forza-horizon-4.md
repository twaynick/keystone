# Using Keystone with Forza Horizon 4

Forza Horizon 4 has no console, so Keystone's help is the **Library**, the **overlay** and, offline only, **mods**.

## Library

All 751 cars, grouped by make, each with its picture, class and Performance Index (PI), value, how to get it, rarity, country and the game's stat bars. Search finds a car by any part of its name. Open a car for its Forza Wiki page in Keystone's browser (**Ctrl+Shift+B** shows it over the game).

Classes go by PI: D (100–500), C (501–600), B (601–700), A (701–800), S1 (801–900), S2 (901–998) and X (999).

"How to get it" is worded as the Forza Wiki has it. "Hard to Find" cars aren't in the Autoshow; look for them in the Auction House, the Festival Playlist or Wheelspins.

## Mods

Mods are off until you turn them on for this game and accept the warning. Forza Horizon 4 saves your progress to your Xbox profile and syncs it whenever you're online, so anything a mod changes (credits, cars, XP, Wheelspins) goes with you into online play, including if you modded in Solo or offline. Playground Games bans accounts it finds cheating. Use mods only playing offline, and only on progress you're prepared to lose.

Load a Cheat Engine table made for your exact game version and store (Steam and Xbox app builds differ) in **Mods**. After a game update an old table shows values as unreadable; it needs updating, nothing is wrong with your game.

## Overlay

Set **Display mode** to Windowed or Borderless in the game's video options so the overlay, timers and browser show on top. A fullscreen game draws over everything.

## Buying it

Forza Horizon 4 was taken off sale at the end of 2024. If you own it, it still installs and plays, and Keystone finds it on Steam or the Xbox app as usual.
