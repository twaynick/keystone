# Using Keystone with Fallout Shelter

Fallout Shelter is single-player with no online play, so mods have no ban risk. Keystone's help here is the **Library**, the **overlay**, and **mods** from a Cheat Engine table.

## Library

- **Dwellers:** all 53 legendary dwellers and the rare ones, with their SPECIAL, and for legendaries the weapon and outfit they arrive with and which game they're from.
- **Weapons:** every weapon by type, with its damage, average damage, rarity and sell price.
- **Outfits:** SPECIAL bonuses, rarity and sell price, including power armor.
- **Pets:** the bonus each breed gives at common, rare and legendary, and its legendary name.
- **Rooms:** what SPECIAL each one uses, the population it unlocks at, and what it costs to build and upgrade.

Search finds anything by name. Each entry has a picture and opens its Nukapedia page in Keystone's browser (**Ctrl+Shift+B** shows it over the game).

## Legendary dwellers checklist

The Overlay tab has a **Legendary dwellers** checklist with all 53. Tick them off as lunchboxes and quests bring them in. It's in the overlay and on your phone too.

## Mods

Load a Cheat Engine table made for your game version in **Mods**. Tables for Fallout Shelter typically cover things like caps, resources, lunchboxes and Nuka-Cola Quantum.

**Back up first.** Your vaults are in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FalloutShelter` (Vault1.sav to Vault3.sav, with .bkp copies). Copy that folder somewhere safe before modding. A value pushed too far can break a vault, and with cloud saves on, a broken vault can sync to your other devices.

## Overlay

Run the game in a window rather than fullscreen so the overlay, timers and browser show on top. Timers suit wasteland explorers and quest teams: set one for when they'll be back.
