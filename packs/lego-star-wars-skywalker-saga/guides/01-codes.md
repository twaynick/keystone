# Codes in LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga

## Entering a code

1. Pause the game.
2. Open **Extras**: the tab with a key and a plus symbol.
3. Choose **Enter Code** and type the code exactly as shown.

The character or ship unlocks straight away.

Other cheats, like stud multipliers, aren't codes in this game: find **Datacards** while exploring, then switch the cheats on under **Extras** after paying for them with studs.

## The codes

Keystone lists 20 codes under **Library → Codes**, each with a Copy button. Only codes that at least two separate guides agree on are included, so a typo in one guide can't send you a dud.

The **Codes entered** checklist (Overlay tab) lets you tick off characters and vehicles as you unlock them; pick it as the checklist to see it in the overlay and on your phone.

## Overlay

Run the game in **borderless** or **windowed** mode for Keystone's overlay and browser (**Ctrl+Shift+B**) to show on top.
